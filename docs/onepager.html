The Question

核心问题

Riders who skip a trip on a rainy day may still shift their later travel plans. We study how short-run weather shocks affect a platform's pricing and fleet rebalancing in a two-period model. The operator commits to a fixed price and a rebalancing capacity. Under the maintained equilibrium, the model delivers a three-region dispatch rule: static pricing in mild weather, dynamic rebalancing after either demand shock, and capacity expansion when weather sensitivity exceeds a threshold.
